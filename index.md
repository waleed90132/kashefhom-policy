# 🇬🇧 English Version

## Privacy Policy & Terms of Service
**App Name:** Kashefhom: Kuwait Number Book  
**Package Name:** `com.kashefhom.callerid`  
**Contact Email:** `kashefhom.dev@gmail.com`  
**Last Updated:** August 2026  

---

### 1. Zero Contact Scraping Guarantee 🛡️
Welcome to **Kashefhom**. Your digital privacy and device security are our utmost priority.

> **Privacy Declaration:**  
> Kashefhom operates strictly as an independent directory search client. **We do NOT read, sync, collect, upload, share, or store your personal phone contacts, address book, or personal call logs.** The application requires only internet access to execute queries initiated directly by you.

---

### 2. Data Processing Overview 🔍

| Data Category | Collection Status | Purpose & Handling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Contacts** | ❌ **Never Collected** | No address book permissions are requested or used. |
| **Search Queries** | ⚙️ **Temporary Processing** | Numbers queried manually are sent securely via HTTPS to return results. |
| **Call Logs & SMS** | ❌ **Never Accessed** | We do not monitor, read, or process call logs or SMS messages. |
| **Technical Telemetry** | 📊 **Non-Personal Data** | Basic crash logs and OS version data to maintain app performance. |

---

### 3. Permissions & Network Security 🔒
* **`android.permission.INTERNET`:** Strictly utilized to communicate securely with directory servers.
* **Encrypted Transmission:** All queries are transmitted over **HTTPS (TLS 1.3)** protected by Cloudflare security architecture to prevent interception.

---

### 4. Third-Party Advertising 📢
Kashefhom may display advertisements provided by compliant ad partners (such as AppLovin MAX, Unity Ads, Start.io) to support operational infrastructure. These providers may process anonymous, non-personally identifiable advertising IDs strictly under standard privacy regulations.

---

### 5. Terms of Service & Disclaimer ⚖️
* **Permitted Use:** Designed exclusively for personal, non-commercial directory lookup within Kuwait.
* **Directory Nature:** Information is aggregated from public indexes and directories. Records are provided "as is" without warranties of real-time completeness or 100% accuracy.

---

### 6. Number Removal & Opt-Out (Data Deletion Request) 🗑️
If you wish to remove or delist your public number from the directory:
* Send an email to: **`kashefhom.dev@gmail.com`**
* **Subject:** `Number Removal Request - [Your Number]`
* Requests are processed and delisted within 24 to 48 business hours.

---

### 7. Developer Contact 📬
For feedback, general inquiries, or support:
* **Official Email:** `kashefhom.dev@gmail.com`
